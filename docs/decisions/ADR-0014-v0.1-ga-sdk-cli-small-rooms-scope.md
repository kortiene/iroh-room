# ADR-0014: Scope v0.1 GA to the SDK and CLI for Small Private Rooms (RB-1.1)

- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Status:** Proposed until the merge that introduces this ADR; accepted by
  that merge. Introduces roadmap baseline amendment `RB-1.1`.
- **Amended:** 2026-11-15, OD-1 decided (roadmap amendment `RB-1.2`).
- **Decision owner:** Maintainer
- **Amends:** [`ADR-0012`](ADR-0012-roadmap-completion-before-external-cohort.md)
  and [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md) RB-1.0
- **Related:** `PRODUCTION-READINESS.md`, `docs/releases/v0.1.0-rc.5-production-beta-signoff.md`
  (WAIVER-PBETA-0001..0005), `docs/sdk-coverage.md`, issues #232, #235–#238,
  #248, #250, #265, #268, #293, #306, #313, #343, #344, #349, #162

## Context

RB-1.0 (ADR-0012) made every explicit future deliverable cohort-blocking: the
Tauri desktop app, calls and the SFU stage, the full v2 replica protocol
(Phases C/D/E, including federation), the availability layer, the 10,000-member
bootstrap condition, the large-room gossip overlay, PRD Phase 3 task/agent
events, and the live-pipe extensions. On 2026-11-14 RB-1 had 136 open
execution issues and the first GA could not be reached until all of them
closed.

Meanwhile what already ships (`v0.1.0-rc.5`, a controlled Production Beta) is
a Rust SDK façade (`crates/iroh-rooms`, source/workspace only) and the
`iroh-rooms` CLI built on top of it, for small private rooms: full-mesh transport with the hard
`MAX_ACTIVE_MEMBERS = 5` cap, signed messages, verified files, authenticated
loopback live pipes, and invited agents. Its residual production gaps are a
small, known set: unsigned artifacts with no provenance, no dependency/supply
chain gate, an unpublished SDK (`publish = false`, WAIVER-PBETA-0005), a
content-encryption lifecycle that is implemented but not
reachable from the shipped CLI, no independent security review, two missing
real-network evidence legs, and partial install/upgrade/rollback
qualification.

The maintainer wants a production release of what already ships, as soon as
those gaps are closed, without waiting for the rest of the roadmap. Because the
CLI is built on the SDK, the SDK is part of that release, not a follow-up.

## Decision

Adopt RB-1.1: **v0.1 GA is the Rust SDK (`iroh-rooms` crate) and the
`iroh-rooms` CLI built on it, for small private rooms (the 5-member full-mesh
cap)**. The SDK is published to crates.io together with its dependency closure
(`iroh-rooms-core`, `iroh-rooms-crypto`, and, for the `experimental` online
tier, `iroh-rooms-net`). The CLI ships as signed binaries on the platform
triples that already ship (`x86_64-apple-darwin`, `aarch64-apple-darwin`,
`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, `aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu`).

RB-1.1 introduces three scope classes that sit alongside the RB-1.0 classes:

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `GA-P0` | Must be complete (or, where the row says so, explicitly dispositioned by the maintainer) before the v0.1 GA candidate is tagged. |
| `GA-P1` | Targeted for v0.1 GA. A `GA-P1` row that is not complete at the candidate must be recorded as a dated, named limitation in the GA release notes; it does not silently drop. |
| `POST-GA` | Remains in the roadmap with its RB-1.0 row, dependencies, and evidence unchanged, but no longer blocks the v0.1 GA candidate or its cohort. |

Rows that RB-1.1 does not name keep their RB-1.0 classification and are listed
as **unclassified pending maintainer decision** in `ROADMAP.md`. They do not
block the v0.1 GA candidate unless the maintainer reclassifies them `GA-P0` or
`GA-P1` in a later RB-1.x amendment.

### v0.1 GA P0 gate

1. **CI green on a pinned toolchain** (`RB-GA-001`, new): `verify`, `msrv`,
   and `release-readiness` green on a pinned stable toolchain, with the
   scheduled `release-readiness` workflow re-enabled.
2. **Dependency remediation and supply-chain gate** (`RB-REL-003`, #238):
   RustSec advisories resolved or dispositioned, `cargo-deny` enforced in CI,
   automated dependency updates configured.
3. **Signed releases and provenance** (`RB-REL-001` #237, `RB-REL-002` #248).
4. **Encryption decision** (`RB-SEC-011` #268, `RB-SEC-002` #250,
   `RB-REL-007` #343) — **open decision OD-1, below.**
5. **Independent security review** (`RB-SEC-010` #344, final delta
   `RB-FC-009` #353) — **open decision OD-2, below.**
6. **Install, upgrade, rollback, and uninstall on all four supported
   platforms** (`RB-REL-004` #265, `RB-REL-005` #293, gate `RB-FC-004` #347).
7. **Real-network evidence** (`RB-NET-007` #235, `RB-NET-008` #236, gate
   `RB-FC-005` #348 scoped to Gate A): either the missing legs are measured,
   or the maintainer records a dated acceptance of WAIVER-PBETA-0001/0002 for
   v0.1 GA. Unlike RB-1.0, an explicitly accepted waiver satisfies this item
   for the v0.1 GA candidate only.
8. **SDK publication** (`RB-SDK-002` #313, with `RB-SDK-001` #306 coverage
   reconciliation scoped to the GA surface): the supported crates are published
   with versioning, ownership, and a release procedure. The CLI depends on the
   SDK, so this is a release deliverable, not a follow-up.
9. **Schema-evolution decision** (`RB-DEC-001` #232, ADR-0013 in #360):
   publishing the SDK fixes a public compatibility contract, and `RB-SEC-002`
   and the GA JSON surfaces depend on it.
10. **Candidate gates scoped to the v0.1 GA candidate**: `RB-FC-001`, `-002`,
   `-003`, `-004`, `-005` (Gate A only), `-006` (v1, immutable-vector,
   prior-candidate, and rollback compatibility only; v2 migration is
   `POST-GA`), `-009`, `-010`, `-011`, `-012` (#345, #346, #352, #347, #348,
   #349, #353, #354, #355, #356). "All PRE/COND rows" in their dependency
   expressions reads as "all `GA-P0` rows" for this candidate.

### v0.1 GA P1 targets

- Access and audit: `RB-SEC-001` native invite revocation (#249),
  `RB-SEC-016` audit retention/rotation (#239), `RB-SEC-017` tamper-evident
  audit (#254), `RB-SEC-018` audit export and incident reconstruction (#269).
- Data: `RB-SEC-006` backup/restore (#285) and its drill `RB-FC-008` (#351,
  scoped to backup/restore of the GA storage posture), `RB-SEC-004` /
  `RB-SEC-009` local at-rest encryption (#266, #294; or an explicit ADR-0001
  posture carried into GA with disclosure).
- Shipped admin-fork detector (ADR-0006): `RB-ADM-001` (#230), `RB-ADM-002`
  (#240), `RB-ADM-003` (#260), `RB-ADM-004` (#241).
- SDK/CLI surfaces: `RB-UP-101`..`106` (#255, #256, #257, #258, #259, #270),
  `RB-CLI-001` smoke-check (#261).
- Operations: `RB-REL-006` support and incident procedures (#299).

For the GA candidate, `RB-SEC-006` reads its `RB-SEC-004`/`RB-SEC-005`
dependencies as "the GA storage posture"; `RB-SEC-004` depends on the
unclassified `RB-SEC-003`, so if that row stays unclassified, at-rest
encryption carries into GA as a disclosed ADR-0001 limitation.

  `RB-PIPE-004`.
- PRD Phase 6 calls and SFU: `RB-PRD6-001..007`.
- The v2 replica protocol: `RB-ENTRY-001..007`, `RB-V2C-001..014`,
  `RB-V2D-001..009`.
- Federation and the remaining v2 Phase E architecture: `RB-V2E-001..004`.
- PRD Phase 5 availability/always-on nodes: `RB-PRD5-001..007`.
- The 10,000-member bootstrap condition: `RB-COND-001`.
- The large-room gossip overlay: `RB-NET-001..006`, and the N=40/v2 scale
  gate `RB-FC-007`.
- The desktop OS/architecture matrix: `RB-COND-002` (the v0.1 GA CLI matrix is

`POST-GA` rows remain mandatory for the full roadmap and keep their RB-1.0
dependencies. A later RB-1.x amendment defines the next release that they
target.

## Open decisions for the maintainer

These are recorded, not decided, by this ADR. Each must be resolved by a dated
maintainer decision before the v0.1 GA candidate is tagged.

- **OD-1 — Content encryption at v0.1 GA.** Either (a) make encrypted content
  writes and removal-triggered key rotation reachable from the shipped CLI
  (#268, #250, and their rotation-hardening dependencies), or (b) ship v0.1
  GA with plaintext room content, remove or correct every claim that implies
  otherwise (#343, threat model T27), and carry the gap as a named
  limitation. The choice also decides whether `RB-SEC-012..014` and
  `RB-ADM-005` become `GA-P0`, and which SDK surface the encryption APIs take
  in the published crates.

  **Decided 2026-11-15 (maintainer): option (b).** v0.1 GA ships room content
  as plaintext, encrypted in transit only (iroh QUIC/TLS between admitted
  peers), and says so plainly in the README, `SECURITY.md`, and the threat
  model. End-to-end content encryption and removal-triggered key rotation are
  `POST-GA` and the v0.2 headline. Recorded as roadmap amendment `RB-1.2`:
  `RB-SEC-002` (#250), `RB-SEC-011` (#268), `RB-SEC-012..014` (#280, #286,
  #295), and `RB-ADM-005` (#297) become `POST-GA`; `RB-REL-007` (#343) stays
  `GA-P0` and must carry an encryption-claims entry. The rotation builder
  `build_member_removed_with_rotation` moves from the stable `iroh_rooms::room`
  re-exports to `iroh_rooms::experimental::rotation`, so the published stable
  tier does not promise rotation. OD-2's review scope narrows accordingly: it
  confirms the encryption path is unreachable in the shipped configuration
  rather than reviewing the cryptography.
- **OD-2 — Independent security review.** Who performs the review of the v1
  shipping trust surfaces (#344), its scope, budget and timing, or whether
  v0.1 GA ships with WAIVER-PBETA-0003 explicitly re-accepted and disclosed.
- **OD-3 — External cohort before v0.1 GA.** RB-1.1 keeps ADR-0012's rule
  that the external cohort runs against the tagged candidate before the GA
  decision, re-pointed at the v0.1 GA candidate. The cohort rows
  (`RB-COH-001..008`, #100–#103, #357, #358) are left unclassified until the
  maintainer confirms or amends that.

## Consequences

- The v0.1 GA candidate can be tagged once the `GA-P0` gate passes, without
  waiting for desktop, calls, v2, availability, the overlay, or PRD3 work.
- The SDK's `publish = false` and WAIVER-PBETA-0005 end at v0.1 GA. The crates
  in the publication closure need version requirements on their path
  dependencies and a semver/stability policy for the stable and
  `experimental` tiers.
- ADR-0012's "every cohort-blocking row" rule now reads against the RB-1.1
  classification: `POST-GA` rows carry `No (POST-GA, RB-1.1)` in the
  cohort-blocking column.
- The README, SDK docs, and release notes must keep describing v0.1 GA
  honestly: 5-member rooms, no guaranteed offline delivery, local-only audit, and whatever OD-1
  and OD-2 settle.
- GitHub milestones gain a `v0.1 GA` milestone for `GA-P0`/`GA-P1` issues and
  a `Post-GA` milestone for `POST-GA` issues; unclassified issues stay in
  their RB-1.0 milestones.
- Preservation invariants from ADR-0012 are unchanged: frozen vectors, signed
  artifacts, approved release records, `admin_seq` semantics, and the
  divergence-cache key are not touched by this amendment.

## Alternatives considered

### Keep RB-1.0 and reach GA after the whole roadmap

Rejected by maintainer decision. It puts more than a hundred open issues,
including entire new products (desktop, calls, federation), between users and
a production release of the SDK and CLI that already ship.

### Ship the CLI at GA and publish the SDK later

Rejected by maintainer decision. The CLI is built on the SDK; publishing the
CLI without the SDK would leave its public foundation unversioned and
unsupported.

### Declare rc.5 GA as-is

Rejected. rc.5 ships under five waivers, unsigned artifacts, open RustSec
advisories, and a CI gate that is red on current stable. Those are the `GA-P0`
items above.

### Drop post-GA rows from the roadmap

Rejected. ADR-0012's scope governance still applies: RB-1.1 reclassifies those
rows and does not delete them.
